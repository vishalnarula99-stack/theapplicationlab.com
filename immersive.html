What Is TIL

These universities shaped how the world thinks. We build programmes that let you understand why — from the inside.

The Immersive Lab creates fully tailored academic programmes inside the colleges, seminar rooms, and libraries of Oxford and Cambridge. Every experience is built around genuine intellectual encounter: not what these institutions look like, but how they reason, what they have discovered, and what it means to be part of a tradition that has shaped modern thought.

Our founders read here. That means alumni relationships, college access, and a first-hand understanding of what makes Oxford and Cambridge intellectually distinctive — and how to make that legible and transformative for your group.

"To work inside these colleges is to encounter, viscerally, what sustained intellectual ambition looks and feels like. We built The Immersive Lab so that students, professionals, and leaders from anywhere in the world can have that encounter — and leave having genuinely thought differently."

— The TIL Team

What We Offer

What a TIL programme is made of.

Every programme draws from these elements — selected and sequenced around the intellectual goals you bring to us.

Academic Workshops

Facilitated seminars on AI and ethics, sustainability, Oxbridge preparation, leadership, and futures thinking. Each session is written for your group — not repurposed from a template, not recycled from last month.

Guest Speakers

Academics, researchers, and practitioners matched to your theme and sector. They come prepared for your specific group, not with a standing talk. The conversation is the point.

College Access & Venues

Seminar rooms and dining halls inside historic colleges, secured through our alumni relationships. Working where Newton worked, where Darwin studied — the setting carries intellectual weight that no conference room replicates.

Punting on the River Cam

Guided tours along the Cam with scholarly narration — the colleges, their histories, the discoveries made within them. Less sightseeing; more understanding what you are looking at and why it matters.

Museum & Heritage Tours

Guided visits to Cambridge's research museums — from Darwin's specimens at the Zoology Museum to the Whipple's history of scientific instruments. Discovery explained in its original context.

Formal Hall Dining

Dinner in a historic college dining hall — under portraits of scholars, in a space where the tradition of intellectual conversation is entirely unselfconscious. Available for residential programmes. The discussion it generates tends to outlast the evening.
