Before You Pay Anything

You will have spoken to us twice before you sign up for anything.

Call one · Discovery, with the TAL team · 20 minutes

We get to know you properly: what you're applying for, your deadlines, where you are in the process, and what you're finding most difficult. We'll tell you honestly what kind of support we think would be useful, even if that means you need less support than you initially thought. There's no pressure to enrol. This call is about understanding what you need first.

Call two · Introduction, with your matched mentor · 20 minutes

Meet the mentor we've matched you with, hear about their experience navigating a similar application journey, and get a sense of whether the fit feels right. You only proceed if you're comfortable. If the match doesn't feel right, we'll find another mentor and arrange the same introductory conversation with them.

Both calls are free and there is no obligation to enrol in the mentorship programme after either one.

Questions

A few things people ask before booking.

Will my mentor write my essays?

No, and we would not help if you asked. Your mentor will tell you what is working, what is missing, how your writing is likely to be read by an admissions or scholarship panel, and where your argument or story could be stronger. They will give you detailed feedback and help you think more critically about what you want to say, but the ideas, voice and writing remain yours.

What if the mentor is not right for me?

That's exactly what the introductory call is for. You'll have 20 minutes to meet your matched mentor, ask questions, and decide whether the fit feels right before you enrol. If, at any point later in the mentorship, the match no longer feels right, just let us know. We'll work with you to find a better fit, and you won't lose any sessions because of a mismatch.

Which package should I choose?

Book a complimentary discovery call and talk it through with us. We'll recommend the level of support that actually fits your application, timeline, and needs. We would rather you choose four sessions you'll genuinely use than ten you don't need.

What happens if I do not use my sessions in time?

Sessions expire at the end of the stated validity period, so we recommend choosing a package that realistically matches your application timeline. If something significant changes along the way, please get in touch with us before your sessions expire. We'll always try to understand the circumstances and see what can reasonably be accommodated.

What if I cannot afford the mentorship programme?

We understand that 1:1 mentorship will not be financially accessible to everyone. Unfortunately, we are not able to offer fee waivers, discounts, or mentorship in exchange for services or partnership arrangements. Our mentors are paid for their time and expertise, and we are committed to remunerating them fairly.

However, you do not need to pay for mentorship to be part of TAL. Our free community is open to everyone, where you can access application and scholarship guidance, attend webinars and Lab Clinic Hours, discover Master's, PhD, postdoctoral and scholarship opportunities, and ask questions directly to our community of applicants, scholars and mentors.

Will I be able to contact my mentor between sessions?

Yes. Once your mentorship begins, we'll connect you with your mentor through our formal communication channels, so you can stay in touch between sessions. You're welcome to send brief questions, updates, or clarifications as you work through your application.

More substantial feedback, document reviews, or discussions that require dedicated time should be reserved for your mentorship sessions. This helps us respect our mentors' time while making sure you still have continuity and support throughout the process.

Can I choose my own mentor?

If you have seen someone in our mentor community you would particularly like to work with, let us know during your discovery call. We'll check their suitability and availability. If they are not available, we'll suggest the closest possible match.

Do I need to have my essays or proposal ready before starting mentorship?

Not at all. You can start much earlier. Some mentees come to us with a complete draft; others begin with an idea, a CV and a list of universities. Your sessions can be used for application strategy, programme choices and developing your narrative before you start writing, as well as reviewing drafts later.

Can I use my sessions for different things?

Absolutely. Your sessions are yours to use where they are most useful. That might mean programme selection in one session, your SOP or research proposal in another, and scholarship or interview preparation later in the process.

Can I add more sessions later?

Yes. You can start with the number of sessions that feels right and add more later if you need them, subject to your mentor's availability. We would rather you start with what you genuinely need than purchase more sessions than you are likely to use.

Will working with TAL guarantee me an admission or scholarship?

No. No mentor or admissions service can guarantee an outcome, and we would be wary of anyone who claims otherwise. What we can offer is experienced, individualised guidance to help you make stronger decisions, communicate your experiences more effectively, and submit an application that is genuinely your own.
